Terms and limitations

The index is a lookup aid for possible source matches, not a legal or enforcement decision system.

01 / STATUS

Independent and non-endorsed

This website is independently operated. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, approved by, or endorsed by the State of Illinois, the Illinois Secretary of State Police, or any other government agency.

02 / PURPOSE

Historical reference only

Results are possible text matches drawn from the February 2024 Illinois Secretary of State Police Offense Code Index. They are not recommendations, charging decisions, probable-cause determinations, legal conclusions, or statements of current law.

Before relying on any result, verify the exact source page, current Illinois Compiled Statutes, amendments, case law, court rules, and applicable agency policy. Reporting codes and practices may vary by agency.

03 / RESPONSIBLE USE

Use professional judgment and authoritative systems

Do not use the index as the sole basis for detention, arrest, charging, citation, reporting, or any action affecting a person’s rights. Do not enter PII, CJI, plates, identifiers, case numbers, or narratives.

Use the website only when safely stationary. It is not an emergency service and must never delay an emergency response or required agency procedure.
