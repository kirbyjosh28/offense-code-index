Sources and methodology

Transparent provenance for an independently extracted, historical February 2024 reference.

01 / SOURCE PUBLICATION

Illinois Secretary of State Police · February 2024

The 953 displayed offense records were extracted from the Illinois Secretary of State Police Offense Code Index dated February 2024. The government publication is the source; this website is an independent, non-endorsed presentation of selected contents.

Open the externally published source PDF in a new tab.

02 / METHOD

Lookup and source-page mapping

The site provides browser-based text matching over extracted descriptions and codes. Search output represents possible source matches, not legal relevance or a recommended offense. Each record links to its exact page in the source publication.

A versioned manifest records the source URL, retrieval and publication dates, extraction version, corpus checksum, record count, page-mapping checks, and review dates. The original PDF artifact checksum has not been independently verified; users should compare any consequential result with the linked official publication.

03 / CURRENCY

Not a statement of current law

The February 2024 publication may be incomplete, superseded, or inconsistent with later statutes, rules, cases, or agency practices. Verify the current ILCS, authoritative source material, and agency policy before citation or operational use.
